About Us

The next chapter
in our journey

Hanova was founded in the year 2025 as the next chapter in our journey. Building on the foundation of our freelance predecessor, Harry Designs, we rebranded to Hanova to represent our growth into a multi-disciplinary technology firm. Today, we combine years of design intuition with advanced software engineering and data science to provide comprehensive solutions for individuals and corporations alike.

Our Services

Everything your business needs, built by one cohesive team.

01

Visual Graphic Design & Branding

Our creative studio transforms ideas into visual identities for businesses and individuals alike. We handle everything from corporate logos and company profiles to t-shirts, banners, and personalised event programs, supported by professional photo and video editing.

02

Social Media Management

We bridge the gap between your brand and your audience through strategic digital storytelling. Our team manages your online presence by creating engaging content and maintaining consistent growth across all your social platforms.

03

Portfolio Creation & Management

We curate and manage professional portfolios that showcase your best work to the world. Whether you need a digital gallery or a physical look book, we handle the design and regular updates so your professional profile always remains current and competitive.

04

Data Analysis & Visualisation

We turn complex datasets into clear strategic roadmaps using Python, SQL, and Power BI. By cleaning, analysing, and visualising your information, we provide interactive reports that empower your company to make informed, data-driven decisions.
